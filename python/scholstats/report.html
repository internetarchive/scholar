The stats in this report are pulled from a variety of sources and collected once a day as cumulative totals. The code runs on wbgrp-svc500.us.archive.org and can be found on gitlab.

As of right now the graphs are largely plotted day by day. Once we have more data (>60 days worth) I'll tweak the graphs to show different scales of time.

Sandcrawler (SPN)

These two graphs reflect daily use of Save Page Now. The first graph shows the total requests made to SPN per day broken down by successes (PDF acquired) and misses; the second graph shows a breakdown of the most common errors that lead to missed PDFs. Right now errors are included if they average 50 occurences per day or more; once we have more data I'll tweak that.

common spn errors
no-pdf-link no link to PDF found from initial seed
spn2-cdx-lookup-failure SPN result was not found in CDX. this means CDX live is lagging behind.
blocked-cookie SPN failed and it appeared to be because of a lack of a particular cookie
no-capture Tried to find resource in WBM but WBM redirected us too many times
spn2-backoff we oversaturated the SPN API and our request was rejected
terminal-bad-status crawl led to a 4xx or 5xx
skip-url-blocklist url was on our blocklist, did not attempt capture
