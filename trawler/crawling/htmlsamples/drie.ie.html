Title
ARG 2022: Topographic and Geophysical Survey of Lurigethan Promontory Fort, County Antrim, Northern Ireland
Description

The aim of the project was to gain new insights into the nature and extent of prehistoric settlement activity on the inland promontory of Lurigethan, ultimately to facilitate a better understanding of its role within the Bronze Age settlement landscape of the Antrim Plateau and Glens.
